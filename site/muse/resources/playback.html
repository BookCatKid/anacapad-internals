muse resources: Playback & sessions ​

Everything about what is playing right now: the transport controls behind play, pause, skip and seek, the playback sessions the app creates to manage queues and sources, plus metadata reporting and the sleep timer. These ops are the JSON equivalent of the classic AVTransport service.

playback ​

Playback control over the modern API: the core remote of play, pause, skip, seek, and source selection expressed as JSON commands. This is the surface the current app actually drives when you press buttons on the now-playing screen, the modern counterpart of the classic transport commands.
