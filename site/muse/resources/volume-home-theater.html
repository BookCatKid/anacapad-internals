muse resources: Volume & home theater ​

Volume control at both player and group level, plus the home-theater surface: TV input switching, HDMI-CEC and ARC status, audio-delay and surround controls, and the pinewood handshake the soundbar uses with its satellites. Roughly the RenderingControl, GroupRenderingControl and HTControl services re-expressed as JSON.

playerVolume ​

Per-player volume over the modern API: a single speaker's volume and mute as JSON resources. It's the modern twin of the classic rendering-control commands, behind the per-room sliders.
