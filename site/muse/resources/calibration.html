muse resources: Calibration & positioning ​

Room calibration and speaker positioning: Trueplay tuning runs, trueroom measurements, mic-based room detection and positional setup for home-theater satellites. These ops drive the same calibration engine the tuning tones in the firmware artifacts feed into.
