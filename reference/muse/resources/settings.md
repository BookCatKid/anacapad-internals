# muse resources: Settings

Player settings and their effective resolved values: names, EQ, LED state, button lock, loudness and the rest. The effectiveSettings resource is a read-mostly merged view the firmware computes over several backing stores. SystemProperties covers a slice of this over SOAP.

## `settings`

General settings over the modern API: the player's configuration as readable and writable JSON settings rather than one command per knob. It's the consolidated settings surface the app prefers.
